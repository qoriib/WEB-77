Daily Glow Routine 🌞

Keep things sweet and simple with this step-by-step daily skincare routine. Pair it with your favorite playlists and glow all day.

Steps to Follow

  1. Cleanse softly with your go-to Emina face wash.
  2. Prep skin with a hydrating toner or essence.
  3. Target concerns using a brightening serum.
  4. Seal moisture with a lightweight moisturizer.
  5. Protect daily with sunscreen every morning.

Tips from Our Squad 💕

  • Pair sunscreen with a cute bucket hat for extra style.
  • Keep lip balm handy to refresh before video calls.
  • Stay hydrated with fruit-infused water.
  • Do a mini dance break to keep your glow going.
